# Contributing to Your Interview Knowledge Base

## Adding a Question

1. Find the appropriate topic.
2. Add the question to the index.
3. Use `templates/question-template.md`.
4. Add a concise 30–60 second answer.
5. Add detailed explanation and follow-ups.
6. Record company/date if it came from a real interview.
7. Update confidence after reviewing.

## Naming

Use lowercase filenames with hyphens:

`angular-signals.md`

Avoid:

`Angular Signals Final NEW.md`

## Quality Checklist

- Is the answer technically correct?
- Can I explain it without reading?
- Can I give a real-world example?
- Can I answer at least 2 follow-ups?
- Have I documented trade-offs?
